Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Restore the new iPhone from a device backup. You can also use Profile > Export my data to save a copy of your preferences, recipes, plans and grocery lists.

Why did two ingredients not combine on my grocery list?

Dinnermap only combines ingredients when it is confident they are the same item in compatible units. For example, "1 can of tomatoes" and "400 g tomatoes" stay separate, and "to taste" amounts are never added to measured ones. You can edit any item.

How do I remove a recipe-generated grocery item?

Swipe it, or open it and choose Remove from list. Removed items can be restored from the bottom of the list, or choose Reset to plan from the list menu.

How do I delete everything?

Profile > Clear all data.

Are the allergen labels guaranteed?

No. They come from each recipe's ingredient list. Always check packaging if you have an allergy.
